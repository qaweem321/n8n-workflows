# Invoice data extraction with AI

Upload a supplier invoice as a PDF. The AI copies out the supplier, invoice number, dates and
amounts; the workflow checks the math and required fields, then emails accounts the details with a
CSV row ready for Excel, Google Sheets or accounting software. Invoices whose totals don't add up,
or that are missing details, are marked **Needs review** so nobody pays a wrong amount.

## How it works

```text
Upload invoice → Read PDF text → Settings → Extract details with AI → Check the numbers → Spreadsheet row → Make CSV file → Email accounts
                                                     ↑
                                              Local AI model
```

1. **Upload invoice**: n8n's built-in form at `http://localhost:5678/form/invoice` (PDF only).
2. **Read PDF text**: pulls the text out of the PDF (up to 5 pages).
3. **Settings**: your company name and the accounts inbox.
4. **Extract details with AI**: copies supplier, number, dates, currency, subtotal, tax and total
   into JSON. It is told to copy numbers exactly and never correct them.
5. **Check the numbers**: plain code, not AI. Checks subtotal + tax = total, required fields and
   valid dates, marks overdue invoices, and formats dates as "Oct 31, 2026" (UTC) so they are never
   ambiguous. Unit tests: `tests/invoice-extraction.test.mjs`.
6. **Spreadsheet row** and **Make CSV file**: one row with fixed columns: Supplier, Invoice number,
   Invoice date, Due date, Currency, Subtotal, Tax, Total (two decimals), Status (Ready to pay, Overdue or Needs review).
7. **Email accounts**: subject starts with `[CHECK]` when something is wrong and ends with
   `(OVERDUE)` when the due date has passed.

## Credentials

| Node | Credential | Local default | In production |
| ---- | ---------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Local AI model | OpenAI API | `Local AI (llama.cpp)`, free model on your machine | Your OpenAI key, or the Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini chat model node |
| Email accounts | SMTP | `Mailpit (local test inbox)` at http://localhost:8025 | Your SMTP provider |

## Try it

1. Turn the workflow on in n8n.
2. Open http://localhost:5678/form/invoice and upload one of the PDFs in [samples/](samples).
   `brightpixel-design.pdf` has a wrong total on purpose.
3. Open http://localhost:8025 to see the email and CSV (the local AI takes 20 to 50 seconds).

The sample invoices are fictional and generated by `scripts/demo/make-sample-invoices.mjs`.

## Limits

- Scanned invoices (photos, image-only PDFs) have no text layer. Add an OCR step first, or use a
  vision model such as Claude, which can read the PDF directly.
- Line items are not extracted, only the totals. Add a `line_items` key to the prompt if you need them.

## Ideas to extend

- Pick up invoices automatically from an email inbox (IMAP or Gmail trigger) or a Google Drive folder.
- Append the row to Google Sheets or Airtable, or create a bill in QuickBooks or Xero.
- Ask the approver on Slack for anything over a set amount.
